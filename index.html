xKnight

A fast-paced action RPG. Slash through 51 levels, beat 12 unique bosses, collect coins and upgrade your knight. Free, in your browser.

Play nowFullscreen

Sound starts after your first click. On a phone, use the on-screen joystick, or tap Fullscreen for the best view.

Features

51 levelsTen worlds that get harder, ending in a final boss rush.
12 unique bossesLeaps, bone charges, lightning storms and bullet rings, each with its own look.
UpgradesSpend coins at the blacksmith on damage, hearts, speed, reach and attack speed.
ArmoryUnlock 8 skins, 12 swords and 6 slash effects as you climb.
MedalsEarn achievement medals for completing level 50 and level 51.
CheckpointsYour progress saves in your browser after every level.

Controls

MoveWASD or arrow keys
SlashSpace, J or left-click
DashShift, K or right-click (brief invincibility)
Pause / MusicP or Esc / M
TouchOn-screen joystick, Slash and Dash buttons
